Project 2 · Recording Guide

One screen at a time.

The evidence you show and the words you read now advance together. You do not need to find report pages, code functions or terminal windows while speaking.

1. Choose a video above.
2. Read the narration on the right while showing the evidence on the left.
3. In your screen recorder, select only the white evidence panel on the left.
4. Finish the paragraph, then click Next screen. Both panels update together.
5. Stop your recorder after the last paragraph.

This guide does not record your screen. Use your screen recorder with microphone enabled. To show evidence across the entire window, choose Hide narration and read the matching .txt script from paper, a phone or another display. All evidence is embedded; no internet or app startup is required.
