# Frozen proposal for future paper research - no outcomes claimed

This is a prospective research proposal written after the historical replay. It
cannot retroactively turn that replay into an untouched holdout. Approval and an
independently timestamped freeze must precede a future start. No pilot has begun.

## Decision and hypothesis

Question: does the existing constrained minimum-estimated-volatility rule reduce
subsequent net return volatility relative to the same-universe equal-weight rule
without unacceptable concentration, turnover or evidence failures? The mechanism
is covariance-based diversification, not an assumed ability to predict stock returns.

Retain NVDA/MSFT/TSM/QCOM, 36 monthly returns, quarterly target trades, the existing
10% historical-mean feasibility hurdle, long-only/full investment and 5-point grid.
Equal weight is the primary baseline; inverse volatility is secondary. No new
signal, universe, constraint or estimation window may replace this design after
future outcomes are visible without registration as a new hypothesis.

## Timing and implementation proposal

At each future quarterly review, retain original daily source responses, retrieval
time and hashes. Compute only with observations demonstrably available at that
review. Freeze the intended orders before their paper execution; use a subsequent
eligible trading session with at least one full session of information buffer.
Require an auditable timestamp/price convention and defer if timing is unverifiable.
Do not pretend a current adjusted history reconstructs earlier intraday knowledge.

Use 10-bps one-way cost as the base study assumption and retain 25/50-bps stress
comparisons. Include initial deployment, buys plus sells and a self-financing fee.
Costs do not establish live fills. Keep a full ledger of missing inputs, decisions,
targets, drifted holdings, trades, overrides and all alternatives inspected.

## Evaluation and escalation

Observe a proposed 12 months of paper results; no performance is supplied here.
Primary descriptive metric: candidate/equal-weight ratio of sample monthly net
volatility, annualized consistently. Report net cumulative returns and drawdowns
alongside the ratio rather than optimizing a composite score after observation.
Twelve returns provide weak statistical precision and do not by themselves establish
deployable skill. Preserve costs, uncertainty and conditions even if results improve.

The research analyst reviews quarterly. Quarantine on provenance/timing failure,
infeasibility, target movements above 15 percentage points between consecutive
quarterly recommendations, or trailing 12-month net volatility above 22% once
12 returns exist. No threshold relaxation after a breach. Record actual effects
of quarantine separately from an uninterrupted strategy comparison.

The committee reviews any proposed reopening. Positive paper results do not
automatically authorize deployment: require a suitable client mandate, concentration
policy, executable cost/liquidity evidence and independent review. A failed primary
risk comparison means stop or register a genuinely new hypothesis, not rename it.
