comparison baseline. The original seven files are preserved unchanged and hashed.

evidence; it is not backdated or described as the original checkpoint submission.

comparisons, costs, sensitivities, constraints, monitoring, liquidity proxy and

or an AI-off authorship history. Original source names/paths remain in provenance
and archived source files. Presentation labels do not modify original records.
