Overall minimums shown: Decision-ready 270; Strong 255; Developing 210; Minimal 120; Absent/unusable 0. These are rubric level thresholds, not a separate letter-grade scale or a score prediction.

For the top level, the observed rubric expects:

- A specific user, action, horizon, constraints, honest A/B boundary and committee request, with evidence that affects the decision.
- Traceable material inputs with dates, definitions, transformations and exclusions, including handling of revisions, licensing and privacy.
- Correct financial conventions, units, benchmarks, constraints and scenarios, with a defensible action, risks and reversal conditions.
- A clean user's reproducible visible result, coherent code/data flow and safe handling of errors, dependencies and credentials.
- Known answers, a complete genuine human prediction-first locked changed-input record, expected/actual decision effect, meaningful failure/robustness tests, AI-output verification and cold-run evidence.
- Accurate, concise, mutually consistent and accessible reports, README, manifest, transcripts and three videos with distinct purposes.

The Developing validation descriptor specifically identifies an inadequately challenged locked record/high-risk claim as a material gap. The Minimal problem-definition descriptor includes absent or nonresponsive Edition A evidence.
